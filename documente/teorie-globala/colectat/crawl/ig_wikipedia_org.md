Ịpịpị bụ usoro gbatịpụrụ agbatị, nke a na-eme site n'iji aka ọ bụla 'pịa' eriri n'ime bọọdụ mkpịsị aka, si otú a na-emepụta ndetu legato. Ịpịpị n'ozuzu na-agụnye mwepu ma ọ bụ hama-ons. Dịka ọmụmaatụ, onye na-agụ guitar nke aka nri nwere ike ịpịa na mberede ("hama") na mkpịsị aka nri nke aka nri iri na abụọ na, na mmegharị nke iwepụ mkpịsị aka ahụ, dọpụta ("dọrọ") otu eriri ahụ kpasuru iwe na iwe nke asatọ site na obere mkpịsị aka aka ekpe ha. A ga-ewepụ mkpịsị aka a n'otu ụzọ ahụ, na-adọpụ na mgbakasị nke ise. Ya mere, a na-akpọ ndetu atọ ahụ (E, C na A) n'otu n'otu n'otu n'otu dị mfe maka onye ọkpụkpọ.
Ọ bụ ezie na a na-ahụkarị ịpị na Guitar eletrik, ọ nwere ike itinye aka na ihe fọrọ nke nta ka ọ bụrụ ngwá egwu ọ bụla, e mepụtara ọtụtụ ngwá egwu kpọmkwem iji jiri usoro ahụ. A haziri Bunker Touch-Guitar (nke Dave Bunker mepụtara na 1958) maka usoro ahụ, mana ya na ogwe aka iji jide aka nri n'ọnọdụ guitar. Chapman Stick (nke Emmett Chapman mepụtara na mbido afọ 1970) bụ ngwá ọrụ e mere maka ịpị aka, ọ dabere na usoro ịpị mkpịsị aka abụọ nke Free Hands nke Chapman mepụta na 1969 ebe aka ọ bụla na-abịarute fretboard na mkpịsị ụkwụ kwụ ọtọ na frets. E mere[Hamatar](https://ig.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamatar?action=edit&redlink=1 "Hamatar \(ibé a adịgịhị adị\)"), Mobius Megatar, Box Guitar, na Solene maka otu usoro ahụ. A na-arụkwa NS / NS/Stick na Warr Guitar maka ịpị, ọ bụ ezie na ọ bụghị naanị ya. harpejji bụ ngwá egwu a na-akpọ n'elu ihe, dị ka keyboard, na mkpịsị aka na-ejikarị eriri kama ịpị. Ngwá egwu ndị a niile na-eji eriri na-erughị guitar ọkọlọtọ, na obere ọrụ iji mee ka eriri ahụ dịkwuo mfe.
Tapping adịwo n'ụdị ụfọdụ ma ọ bụ ọzọ ruo ọtụtụ narị afọ. Niccolò Paganini (1782-1840) jiri usoro yiri nke ahụ mee ihe na violin, na-eji ụta na-awụli elu nke pizzicato aka ekpe na-akụ eriri ahụ. Paganini weere onwe ya dị ka onye na-akpọ ụbọ akwara karịa onye na-eme violin, ma n'ezie dee ọtụtụ egwu maka ụbọ akhara, nke a ma ama bụ "Grand Sonata for Violin and Guitar". A naghị eme ihe ndị ọ na-akpọ n'oge a, ọ bụ ezie na egwu violin ya na-enwe ọtụtụ arụmọrụ. Ụfọdụ ndị ọkà mmụta egwu kwenyere na o dere sonatas violin 37 ya na guitar wee degharịa ha maka violin. A maara Paganini nke ọma n'ụlọ oriri na ọṅụṅụ ndị na-agagharị agagharị, ọ ga-abụ na ọ na-ahụ usoro guitar gypsy site na Romani, "gypsies". Ọ họọrọ ịkpọ guitar ya maka ndị ahịa ụlọ oriri na ọṅụচল kama ịkpọ ndị na-ege ntị n'ụlọ egwu.
N'ọnwa Ọgọstụ n'afọ 1969, Chapman mepụtara ụzọ ọhụrụ nke aka abụọ na-eji aka abụọ ejide n'olu site n'akụkụ dị iche iche, si otú a na-eme ka aka ọ bụla nwee ike ịha nhata. Iji bulie usoro ahụ, Chapman mepụtara guitar eletrik 9 nke ọ kpọrọ "Electric Stick" (ma mesịa nụchaa ya dị ka Chapman Stick), ngwá ọrụ a raara nye kachasị ewu ewu. Ụdị Chapman na-edozi mkpịsị aka aka aka nri n'otu akụkụ na frets, dịka n'aka ekpe, mana site n'akụkụ nke ọzọ nke olu. Nchọpụta ya dugara na ikike zuru oke, na ngwá ọrụ ọhụrụ, Chapman Stick, na usoro "Free Hands" ya. Chapman nwere mmetụta n'ọtụtụ ndị na-akpọ ụbọ akwara, gụnyere Steve Lynch nke Autograph, na Jennifer Batten.
Randy Resnick (nke otu Pure Food and Drug Act, nke n'otu oge gosipụtara Mandel) jiri usoro aka abụọ na-emetụ aka mee ihe n'ụzọ dị ukwuu na arụmọrụ ya na ndekọ ya n'etiti afọ 1969 na 1974. A kpọtụrụ Resnick aha na akụkọ ndụ Eddie Van Halen maka enyemaka ya na usoro aka abụọ.[[3]](https://ig.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E1%BB%8Ak%E1%BB%A5p%E1%BB%A5_ihe#cite_note-3)N'izo aka na Resnick na Richard Greene And Zone na Whisky a Go-Go na 1974, Lee Ritenour kwuru na magazin Guitar Player na Jenụwarị 1980 na "Randy bụ onye mbụ na-akpọ ụbọ akwara m hụrụ onye dabeere n'ụdị ya niile na ịpị. " Resnick dekwara site na iji usoro ahụ na 1974 na John Mayall & the Bluesbreakers album Latest Edition ma kwuo na ọ na-anwa imepụtaghachi legato nke John Coltrane's "Sheets of Sound".
Ụfọdụ ndị na-egwu guitar nwere ike ịhọrọ ịpị site na iji ọnụ dị nkọ nke ịhọrọ kama mkpịsị aka iji mepụta ndetu ngwa ngwa, nke siri ike karịa nke trilling, na usoro a maara dị ka pick tapping or pick trilling. Iji nhọpụta ahụ na-eme ka ọsọ ọsọ ọsọ site na 'majijiji' (ma ọ bụ ijide nke ọma) ndị Guitarists nkwojiaka dị ka Joe Satriani na John 5 Lowery amarala iji ya, na Lowery na-akpọ ya "Spider-Tap".
A na-emepụta harmonics apịaji site n'iji aka onye ọkpụkpọ jide ihe ndetu, na iji aka onye ọkpụkpọ na-akụtu ihe iri na abụọ na-ewetu site na ndetu ahụ (ya bụ, ihe edeturu na fret nke 4 nke eriri A na-akụ na 16th fret nke eriri A) . Kama iji aka nri na-akụtu na ịdọpụ ya, a na-emepụta ihe nkwekọ site n'iji mkpịsị aka kụgbuo iwe ahụ. Enwere ike ịnụ usoro ịme ọpịpị a na egwu Van Halen "Women in Love" na "Dance the Night Away". Early Metallica bassist Cliff Burton jikwa ihe nkwekọ tapped na guitar bass na mpempe akụrụngwa ya ama ama "(Anesthesia) Pulling Teeth"
