# Book Title:Open Music Theory
Book Description:_Open Music Theory_ is a natively-online open educational resource intended to serve as the primary text and workbook for undergraduate music theory curricula._OMT2_ provides not only the material for a complete traditional core undergraduate music theory sequence (fundamentals, diatonic harmony, chromatic harmony, form, 20th-century techniques), but also several other units for instructors who have diversified their curriculum, such as jazz, popular music, rhythm, counterpoint, and orchestration. This version also introduces a complete workbook of assignments.

**_Open Music Theory Version 2 (OMT2)_** is an open educational resource intended to serve as the primary text and workbook for undergraduate music theory curricula. As an open and natively-online resource,_OMT2_ is substantially different from other commercially-published music theory textbooks, though it still provides the same content that teachers expect from a music theory text.
_OMT2_ has been designed inclusively. For us, this means broadening our topics beyond the standard harmony and atonal theory topics to include fundamentals, musical form, jazz, pop, rhythm, and orchestration. And within those traditional sections of harmony and atonal theory, the authors have deliberately chosen composers who represent diverse genders and races. The book is accessible. And perhaps most importantly, the book is completely free and always will be.
