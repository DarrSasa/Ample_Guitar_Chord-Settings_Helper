Guitar  
| [String instrument](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/String_instrument "String instrument")([plucked](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pizzicato "Pizzicato"), nylon stringed guitars usually played with fingerpicking, and steel-, etc. usually with a[pick](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plectrum "Plectrum").) (a regularly tuned guitar) The**guitar** is a[string instrument](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/String_instrument "String instrument")which is played by plucking the strings. The main parts of a guitar are the body, the fretboard, the headstock and the strings. Guitars are usually made from wood or plastic. Their strings are made of steel or nylon. The guitar strings are plucked with the[fingers](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finger "Finger")and[fingernails](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fingernail "Fingernail")of the right hand (or left hand, for left handed players), or a small pick made of thin plastic. This type of pick is called a "[plectrum](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plectrum "Plectrum")" or guitar pick. The left hand holds the neck of the guitar while the fingers pluck the strings. Different finger positions on the fretboard make different notes. There are many different types of guitars, classified on how they are made and the type of music they are used for. All traditional types of guitar have a body which is hollow. This makes the sound of the strings louder, and gives the guitar its[quality](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quality "Quality"). This type of guitar is called "acoustic". (An _acoustic_ instrument is one that makes its own[dynamics](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamics_\(music\) "Dynamics \(music\)").) Most guitars have[six](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Six "Six")strings, but there are also guitars with[four](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four "Four"),[seven](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven "Seven"),[eight](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eight "Eight"),[ten](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ten "Ten"), or[twelve](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve "Twelve")strings. More strings make the instrument sound fuller. The neck of a guitar has bars or marks called[frets](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fret "Fret"). Frets help a guitarist know where to put his or her fingers to get the right[pitch](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitch_\(music\) "Pitch \(music\)")when playing. Standard tuning defines the string pitches as E, A, D, G, B, and E, from lowest (low E2) to highest (high E4). Standard tuning is used by most guitarists, and frequently used tunings can be understood as variations on standard tuning. ## Different kinds of guitars
Modern guitars come in four main types. The[classical guitar](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_guitar "Classical guitar")is used for classical music. The term[acoustic guitar](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acoustic_guitar "Acoustic guitar")is generally used for a guitar used for popular music, even though a classical guitar is also an acoustic instrument. There are many other different types of acoustic guitars from different parts of the world. A special electric folding travel guitar called the Foldaxe (briefly manufactured by Hoyer in 1977) was invented for Chet Atkins (in Atkins' book "Me and My Guitars") by inventor and guitarist Roger Field, featuring a built-in way to keep the string tension and tuning the same even when folded, and ready to play when unfolded. Atkins demonstrated his several times on US television, and also on[The Today Show](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Today_Show "The Today Show")with Les Paul, who was with him as a guest. Unlike most guitars with six strings, some guitars are made with eight strings. Eight string guitars have two extra[octave](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octave "Octave")strings on the[D](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/D_\(musical_note\) "D \(musical note\)")and[G](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/G_\(musical_note\) "G \(musical note\)"). This gives the person playing the guitar a large amount of chorus when playing.
## Guitar music
A capo on a guitar Guitars are used in many different genres of music, such as traditional, regional, and folk to modern[punk](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punk "Punk"),[rock](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_music "Rock music"),[metal](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heavy_metal_music "Heavy metal music")or[pop](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_music "Pop music"). Guitars are used as[rhythm](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhythm_guitar "Rhythm guitar")instruments,[lead](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lead_guitar "Lead guitar")instruments, and sometimes both. A**capo** is a device which can be placed on any of the guitar's frets which don't lie on the body itself. This enables the user to change key without changing the tuning of the guitar strings. There are multiple types of capos, some latch around the whole guitar neck and some just clamp onto the back and fret board.
