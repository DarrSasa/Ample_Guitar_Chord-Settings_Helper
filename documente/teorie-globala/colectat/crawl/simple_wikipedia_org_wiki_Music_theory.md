**Theory of Music** is all about trying to understand how existing[music](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music "Music")works and how new music could or should be organized. Someone who makes a special study of music theory is a**[music theorist](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_theorist "Music theorist")**.
Music theory is important for people who perform music because all these things help people to understand the music they are playing.
