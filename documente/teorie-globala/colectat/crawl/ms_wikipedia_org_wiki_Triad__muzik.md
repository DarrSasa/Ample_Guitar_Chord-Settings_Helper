"A triad is a set of notes consisting of three notes built on successive intervals of a third. A triad can be constructed upon any note by adding alternating notes drawn from the scale.... In each case the note that forms the foundation pitch is called the _root_ , the middle tone of the triad is designated the _third_(because it is separated by the interval of a third from the root), and the top tone is referred to as the _fifth_(because it is a fifth away from the root)."
