N'afọ ndị 1950, Leo Fender na George Fullerton mepụtara guitar bass eletrik mbụ e mepụtara n'ìgwè.[[15]](https://ig.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bass_guitar#cite_note-FOOTNOTESlogCoryat1999154-15)Fender Electric Instrument Manufacturing Company malitere imepụta Precision Bass, ma ọ bụ P-Bass, n'ọnwa Ọktoba afọ 1951. Nhazi ahụ gosipụtara "slab" dị mfe (enweghị akụkụ) na otu coil pickup, ihe abụọ ahụ yiri Telecaster. Ka ọ na-erule n'afọ 1957, Precision Bass malitere ịdị ka Fender Stratocaster na akụkụ ahụ ya dị mma maka nkasi obi na pickup gbanwere ka ọ bụrụ ọkara dị iche iche nke kewara coil design.[[16]](https://ig.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bass_guitar#cite_note-16)
Ọtụtụ ụlọ ọrụ ndị ọzọ malitekwara imepụta ụbọ akwara n'afọ ndị 1950. Kay Musical Instrument Company malitere imepụta K162 na 1952. Ọzọkwa na 1956, na ngosi azụmahịa German "Musikmesse Frankfurt", ọdịdị bass pụrụ iche nke[Höfner](https://ig.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C3%B6fner?action=edit&redlink=1 "Höfner \(ibé a adịgịhị adị\)")_500/1" id="mw8A" rel="mw:WikiLink" title="Höfner 500/1">Höfner 500/1 nke dị ka viola pụtara na mbụ, nke Walter Höfner, onye na-eme violin nke ọgbọ nke abụọ, wuru site na iji usoro violin.[[23]](https://ig.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bass_guitar#cite_note-23)N'ihi ojiji[Paul McCartney](https://ig.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_McCartney "Paul McCartney"), a maara ya dị ka "Beatle bass".[[24]](https://ig.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bass_guitar#cite_note-FOOTNOTEBaconMoorhouse2016eBook-24)N'afọ 1957, Rickenbacker wepụtara ụdị 4000, bass mbụ iji gosipụta ihe osise olu-site n'ahụ nke olu bụ akụkụ nke osisi ahụ.[[25]](https://ig.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bass_guitar#cite_note-25)E webatara Burns London Supersound na 1958.[[22]](https://ig.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bass_guitar#cite_note-FOOTNOTEBacon2010-22)
