* ["Fretless Zithers" at MinerMusic.com](http://www.minermusic.com/dolceola/fretless_zithers.htm)（[页面存档备份](https://web.archive.org/web/20201112015504/http://www.minermusic.com/dolceola/fretless_zithers.htm)，存于[互联网档案馆](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E4%BA%92%E8%81%94%E7%BD%91%E6%A1%A3%E6%A1%88%E9%A6%86 "互联网档案馆")） English language site dedicated to the chord zither. * ["Zither US"](http://www.zither.us)（[页面存档备份](https://web.archive.org/web/20210122171352/http://www.zither.us/)，存于[互联网档案馆](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E4%BA%92%E8%81%94%E7%BD%91%E6%A1%A3%E6%A1%88%E9%A6%86 "互联网档案馆")） English language site based in the U.S.A.. Contains biographies of early performers and personalities associated with the history of concert and Alpine zithers, mainly in the U.S. Also contacts for resources like instrument maintenance, enthusiast clubs and events.
